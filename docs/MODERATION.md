# Moderation and legal policy (operator handbook)

Not legal advice. Have a lawyer review the public Terms, Privacy Policy and Contributor Agreement before public launch.

## Principles
- The operator hosts community posts; contributors are the authors. The operator does not write, rewrite or "fix" user posts. Staff may hide, remove or restore.
- Moderate by the published Community Guidelines, never by popularity, dislikes, or viewpoint.
- Every staff action is logged with a reason (`moderation_actions`).

## Escalation table
| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Spam | Remove (hide); suspend repeat offenders |
| Personal attack in a comment | Remove comment; warning note |
| Repeated harassment | Suspend (7 / 30 days), then ban |
| Copyright complaint | Hide pending review; respond promptly per DMCA process |
| Serious factual allegation (crime, misconduct) without sources | Hide pending review; contact author for sources |
| Credible threat of violence | Hide immediately; ban; contact law enforcement |
| Person mentioned in a story disputes it | Review; hide if it's an unsupported factual allegation; invite author correction |

## Defamation safeguards
- Every article has an identifiable author; no anonymous posting.
- Opinion ("I think the council made a bad decision") is allowed. Factual allegations ("the mayor stole $50,000") require credible sources linked in the article.
- Accusations of criminal conduct against private individuals without supporting evidence are prohibited.
- People mentioned in articles can reach the operator via the Contact page or the report button.

## Corrections policy
- Authors can add a correction to their own article at any time; it shows as a dated "Correction" box.
- Edits after publishing are kept in revision history (staff-visible).
- Staff may ask an author to correct; if they don't and the error is material, staff may hide the article.

## Copyright / DMCA
1. Register a DMCA designated agent with the U.S. Copyright Office (copyright.gov/dmca-directory), and list the agent on `/legal/dmca`.
2. On a valid notice: hide the item, notify the contributor, log the action.
3. Counter-notice: may restore after the statutory waiting period if no lawsuit is filed.
4. Repeat-infringer policy: accounts with repeated valid notices are banned.
- Contributors may only post original writing and photos they own or have permission to use. No copying articles from other outlets.

## Contributor Agreement (summary of required terms)
Contributor confirms: they own or have permission for the content and media; they are responsible for the accuracy of factual claims; no defamatory, threatening, harassing or unlawful material; they grant the site a non-exclusive license to display, distribute and promote the submission; deleted posts may persist in copies elsewhere; they accept moderation and takedown policies; accounts can be suspended or terminated.

Checkbox at publish: *"I confirm that I have the right to publish this material, that I have read and agree to the Contributor Agreement, and that I understand I am responsible for the content I submit."* Stored with agreement version, user, article, timestamp, IP hash.

## Privacy
- Collect only: email, handle, display name, optional bio and photo.
- 18+ only (avoids COPPA obligations).
- Account deletion removes or anonymizes the user's data.
